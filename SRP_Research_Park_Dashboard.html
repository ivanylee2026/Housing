1Site Overview

Strengths & Opportunities

Major employment centerMore than 150 companies spanning research, technology, and life sciences; living near work offers an important commute advantage.
Diverse renter profilesEarly-career professionals, families, senior researchers, and executives create demand for homes that evolve with life stages.
Open space and trailsAdjacent to the Stanford Foothills and the 2,300-acre open-space network, including the popular Dish Loop.

Challenges & Considerations

🏛
Limited retail and diningNew residents would need an active, walkable retail and dining environment on site.
Auto-oriented environmentExisting circulation favors cars; development should prioritize safe paths and resident mobility.

8Case Study / Lessons Learned
RELATED BRISTOL  •  SANTA ANA, CALIFORNIA
[ Photo ]
3,750 residential units
41 acres  •  2.7 FAR
Includes 350,000 SF of commercial space, 250 hotel rooms, and 200 senior care units.
Phased execution over ~10 years provides flexibility to respond to changing market conditions.
💡 Key Lessons for SRP
  • Research and employment campuses can anchor a self-contained mixed-use district.
  • Offer a progression of housing choices for changing life stages.
  • Introduce retail, connected public space, and walkable amenities early.
