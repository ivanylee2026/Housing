3Positioning & Renter Profile

Typology / Positioning

Recommend a mixed Standard Luxury and Ultra-Luxury low-rise development to emphasize exclusivity and fit into the surroundings.

Renter Profile

Standard Luxury

Young professionals, established professionals.

$250,000 – $350,000 income

Prestige of address, proximity to employment, modern amenities, high-quality finishes, quiet alternative to urban product.

Ultra-Luxury

Established professionals, senior academics, empty nesters / downsizers.

$300,000 – $750,000+ income or substantial net worth

Prestige, privacy, exclusivity, low-density environment, scarcity of comparable product.

Positioning Rationale

Target rents reflect roughly a 25% site premium due to SLAC's prestige on Sand Hill Road. Standard Luxury will serve as market entry with smaller unit types, while Ultra-Luxury units are at a larger scale with elevated finishes and amenities.
