1Site Overview

Strengths & Opportunities

Established Residential IdentityExisting renter base, physical amenity set, and neighborhood character. A new development would not need to build a sense of place from scratch.
Proximity to Stanford Medical and Academic CommunityNear Stanford University Medical Center, School of Medicine, research facilities, graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, medical residents, and faculty.

3Positioning & Renter Profile

Typology / Positioning

Recommend two feasible Oak Creek approaches: a lighter-touch attainable-luxury infill option and a heavier-touch two-tier program pairing Attainable Luxury with Standard Luxury.

Renter Profile

Attainable Luxury

Audience: Early career and young professionals, Stanford affiliates, including law and families.

Financial Status: ~$250,000

Motivation: Attainable rent, proximity to Stanford and transit access.

Standard Luxury

Audience: Young professionals, established professionals, downsizers.

Financial Status: $150,000 – $350,000

Motivation: Proximity to employment, modern amenities, convenience, and green space.

Positioning Rationale

  • Oak Creek benefits from a prestigious Sand Hill Road address and proximity to Stanford.
  • Lighter touch supports attainable premium positioning with lower monthly entry points.
  • Heavier touch broadens the renter base through a two-tier offering that captures the address premium.

💡 Key Lessons for Oak Creek

  • Sand Hill Road address supports premium rents.
  • Lighter-touch approach for simpler near-term execution; heavier-touch approach for larger long-term value capture.
